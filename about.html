Our Story

From Vision to Reality

Founded in 2023 by Abhishek Karki, Trainexa Logistics has rapidly emerged as a transformative force in the Australian logistics landscape. Our name—a fusion of "Trade" and "Nexus"—embodies our core philosophy: being the vital connection point that enables global commerce to flow seamlessly.

We combine artificial intelligence, IoT-enabled tracking, and blockchain documentation with decades of industry expertise to deliver end-to-end supply chain solutions. Our proprietary Trainexa SmartTrack™ system provides real-time visibility across every stage of the supply chain.

With a client retention rate of 97% and a rapidly expanding network spanning 52 countries, Trainexa Logistics has become the partner of choice for organizations seeking operational excellence.
